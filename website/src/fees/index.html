Fee policy

The terms, in plain words.

01
Refund policy
Fees paid for the course at MasterVLSI are strictly non-refundable under any circumstances.
02
Transferability
Fees are strictly non-transferable and cannot be assigned or shifted to another student.
03
Negotiation
Course fees are strictly non-negotiable.
04
Fee adjustment
Fees paid cannot be adjusted against any other course, service, batch or charges.
05
GST invoice / bill
The GST bill / tax invoice can be collected only after complete payment clearance.
06
Re-enrolment after cancellation
Students whose registration is cancelled due to non-payment must re-enrol under the latest/revised fee structure applicable at that time.
07
Timely payments
Students are advised to complete all payments on or before the due dates to avoid interruptions in training and placement activities.
08
GST
Applicable GST will be charged extra as per government regulations.

Important payment policy

If payment is late.

The maximum duration for fee clearance is 2 months from the date of joining. If payment is not completed in that time, these actions follow:

  • Suspension or removal from live classes and recorded classes
  • Access to study materials, portals and systems may be restricted
  • Removal from official WhatsApp / Telegram groups
  • Placement assistance and interview scheduling may be paused
  • Registration may be cancelled without prior notice
  • Certificate issuance will remain on hold until complete fee clearance

Termination

A student who fails to pay even after multiple reminders (maximum two) will be terminated.

After termination, the student needs to re-enrol under the latest fee structure to continue the course.
