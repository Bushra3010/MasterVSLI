Before you come

Common questions.

I'm from a non-VLSI background. Can I still join?

Yes. We start every cohort with a 3-week digital-electronics and Verilog ramp so anyone with a basic CS/ECE foundation can catch up before the main modules begin.

How long is the program?

Six months of structured cohort, plus one year of validity for revisions, mock interviews and post-placement support.

Is the course live or recorded?

Live, mentor-led and capped at small batches. Every session is also recorded so you can revisit any topic on your own timeline.

What does the placement process look like?

Direct alumni referrals to hiring managers at partner companies. Most students close their first offer within 30–45 days of completing the core modules.

What are the fees?

Online and offline training is ₹90,000 + GST, payable in two equal instalments of ₹45,000 + GST. See the Fees page for the complete terms.

Is accommodation available near the institute?

Yes. There are co-living, girls' and boys' PGs within 10 m to 200 m of the institute. The Campus page lists each one with a contact number.
