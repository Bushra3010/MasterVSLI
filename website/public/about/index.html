Methodology

How a MasterVLSI cohort actually runs.

We don't believe in tutorial-style learning. Every concept is taught against a real artefact you build — a UART, a pipelined adder, an AXI verification environment, a UVM testbench. You finish with a portfolio, not just a certificate.
